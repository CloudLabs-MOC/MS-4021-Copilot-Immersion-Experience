# Sales

### Estimated Duration: Minutes

## Overview

In this lab, you’re preparing a regional sales strategy for EV chargers by analyzing performance data and market trends. Your goal is to understand how your company’s sales compare to national EV adoption patterns, identify growth opportunities, and communicate your strategy effectively. Using Microsoft 365 Copilot, you will research key metrics, generate actionable recommendations, and create a professional proposal and presentation to support your sales initiatives.

## Objectives

- Task 1: Copilot Chat
- Task 2: Copilot in Word
- Task 3: Copilot in PowerPoint

## Task 1: Copilot Chat

In this task, you’ll use Copilot Chat to analyze EV market trends, visualize your company’s regional sales data, and generate data-driven recommendations to boost sales performance.

1. Download the following file by clicking on the **Download file** button:

    - [Charger_sales_report_2022-2024.xlsx](https://github.com/MicrosoftLearning/MS-4021-Copilot-Immersion-Experience/raw/master/ResourceFiles/Charger_sales_report_2022-2024.xlsx)

1. Open a browser and navigate to [M365copilot.com](https://m365copilot.com/).

1. Ensure **Web** mode is selected.

   ![](./Images/p6t2p2.png)

1. Let’s start by asking Copilot to research a key metric. In the **Copilot Chat** prompt field, input:

    ```text
    What is the ratio of EV cars to EV chargers by region in the US for the past 3 years? Please show it in a table organized by region.
    ```

    ![](./Images/p4t1p3.png)

1. Now let’s compare national trends to your company’s sales performance. You’ll upload the provided dataset and ask Copilot to visualize the data. In the prompt field, type:

    ```text
    I need to know the quarterly trends for each of our sales regions. Create a quarterly revenue line graph for the past 2 years based on:
    ```

    > **NOTE:** Do not submit the prompt yet. Move to the next step to upload the file.

1. Click **+ Add content and agents (1)**, then choose **Upload images and files (2)**. In the Open window, select **`Charger_sales_report_2022-2024.xlsx` (3)**, the file downloaded at the start of this exercise, and click **Open (4)**. Finally, submit the prompt.

    ![](./Images/p4t1p4.png)

    ![](./Images/p4t1p4(1).png)

1. Review the result provided by the copilot.

    ![](./Images/p4t1p5.png)

1. Let’s go a step further and ask Copilot to generate recommendations in a Word document. In the prompt field, enter:

    ```text
    Based on the trend, suggest two ways I can increase EV charger sales in the Mountain and Midwest regions. Export the recommendations to a Word Document.
    ```

1. Select the **Hyperlink** Copilot provides for the new Word document to open it.

    ![](./Images/p4t1p6.png)

## Task 2: Copilot in Word

In this task, you'll ask Copilot to expand on these strategies and draft proposals on how to implement them.

1. Click on the **Apps (1)** fromt he left navigative apne and then select **Word (2)** under Apps section.

    ![](./Images/p4t2p1.png)

1. In the Word window, click **Upload a file (1)**. In the **Open** dialog box, select **`EV_Charger_Sales_Recommendations.docx` (2)** and click **Open (3)**.

    ![](./Images/p4t2p2.png)

1. Select anywhere in the body of the document and select the **Draft with Copilot** icon. Paste **(1)** the following prompt and then press Enter or click on **Generate (2)** icon:

    ```text
    Draft a detailed proposal on how we could implement each of the strategies outlined in this document. Ensure the plan is actionable and includes resource requirements, timelines, and key stakeholders.
    ```

    ![](./Images/p4t2p3.png)

1. After reviewing the content generated by Copilot, click **Keep it**. If time allows, demonstrate how to refine or adjust the document using Copilot.

    ![](./Images/p4t2p4.png)

1. When you’re done, click the **drop-down (1)** icon in the top-left corner of the page. Enter **EV Sales Proposal (2)** as the file name, then click anywhere outside the field to save it.

    ![](./Images/p4t2p5.png)

1. Click the **Share (1)** drop-down and select **Copy link (2)** to copy the file link. You’ll need it later in the exercise.

    ![](./Images/p4t2p6.png)

    > **Trainer Tip:** Use this step to demonstrate how Copilot builds on prior outputs, refining ideas into a cohesive proposal.

## Task 3: Copilot in PowerPoint

In this task, you’ll use Copilot in PowerPoint to automatically create a presentation from a Word document, transforming written content into well-structured slides with visuals, notes, and layouts.

1. Click on the **Apps (1)** fromt he left navigative apne and then select **PowerPoint (2)** under Apps section.

    ![](./Images/p4t3p1.png)

1. Click on **Create blank presentation**.

    ![](./Images/p4t3p2.png)

1. If the **AI that works for you-Microsoft 365 Premium** pop-up window appears, click on **Close**.

    ![](./Images/p4t3p3.png)

1. Click on the **Copilot (1)** icon and select the **Create a new presentation (2)**.

    ![](./Images/p4t3p4.png)

1. Paste the **`EV Sales Proposal.docx` (1)** link after "Create a presentation from" and select **Send (2)**. The full prompt should look like:

    ```text
    Create a presentation from [Link to EV Sales Proposal.docx].
    ```

    ![](./Images/p4t3p5.png)

1. Click on **Generate slides**. 

    ![](./Images/p4t3p6.png)

1. Copilot starts creating slides from the EV Sales Proposal, offering an outline complete with speaker notes, images, slide layouts, and a General sensitivity label. Click **Keep it**.

    ![](./Images/p4t3p7.png)
